# Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Graph)

Researched 2026-10-21 from Microsoft Learn (Graph, Entra, Exchange, SharePoint docs) and Nango's source and docs. Bracketed tags like [S5] point to the Sources list at the end. "Delegated" means the app acts as a signed-in person, the Microsoft counterpart of Mycel's per-account Google connection. "App-only" means the app acts as itself across the whole tenant. Non-goal 7 rules app-only out.

## 0. Fit with Mycel's model: the decision-relevant points

1. **Admin consent is effectively mandatory.** Microsoft's own permission table marks most delegated scopes "admin consent: No" [S1]. But tenants on Microsoft's managed default consent policy, the default for new tenants, have since July 2025 blocked users from consenting to third-party apps for Mail.\*, Calendars.\*, Contacts.ReadWrite, Tasks.\*, Chat.Read/ReadWrite, OnlineMeetings.\*, MailboxSettings.\*, People.Read, Files.\*.All and Sites.\*.All [S2]. In practice, someone in each customer tenant who can grant consent (a Global Admin, Cloud Application Admin or Application Admin) must grant tenant-wide consent once. That consent is still **delegated**: every call runs as one signed-in member and sees only what that member can see. So it doesn't breach non-goal 7, though "an admin approves the app" is a new onboarding step Google didn't need.
2. **The pending forms mostly map well.** Outlook has real drafts (`createReply` or `POST /messages`, then `send`), and with immutable IDs we can tell "sent in Outlook" from "deleted" [S13][S15]. A calendar event without attendees, with attendees added on approval, works as it does in Google: Graph sends invitations only to changed attendees [S16]. **Word has no suggestion mode in Graph** and Excel has no tracked edits. Teams has no draft. All three need a design.
3. **Shared mailboxes (support@) don't work as "connect the account".** A shared mailbox has no usable password. You reach it through a member's delegated connection, using `Mail.Read.Shared` / `Mail.ReadWrite.Shared` / `Mail.Send.Shared` plus Exchange Full Access and Send As permissions [S18][S19][S20]. Those `.Shared` scopes **can't create change-notification subscriptions** on the shared mailbox [S19], so support@ triggers must poll.
4. **Push triggers have short leases.** Outlook mail, event and contact subscriptions last at most 10,080 min (just under 7 days); Teams subscriptions last 3 days; To Do just under 3 days; OneDrive and SharePoint just under 30 days [S5]. Delegated subscriptions also stop delivering when the user's access token lapses, until the app reauthorizes [S7][S8]. Nango forwards Outlook-style Graph notifications but doesn't create or renew subscriptions [S41].
5. **Teams is half-usable delegated.** Sending a chat or channel message works with user consent (`ChatMessage.Send`, `ChannelMessage.Send`) [S29]. Reading chats needs `Chat.Read`, which the managed policy blocks for users [S2]. Reading channel messages needs `ChannelMessage.Read.All`, admin-consent-only [S1]. The tenant-wide "all messages" endpoints and chat delta are **app-only** [S28][S30]. Teams also caps polling at once a day per resource, so triggers must use subscriptions [S27]. Teams Graph APIs stopped being metered on 2025-08-25 [S33].

## 1. Summary

For a small company on Microsoft 365, Graph covers everything Google Workspace covers in Mycel today, plus Teams. One host, `graph.microsoft.com`, with one OAuth app and one token serving mail, calendar, contacts, files, Excel, To Do, Planner, Teams and OneNote [S40].

Workflows it most enables:

1. **Inbox triage and drafted replies** in a person's mailbox or a shared mailbox (support@, info@): read, categorize, flag, move, draft a reply, approve and send.
2. **Scheduling**: read free/busy, put a proposed meeting on the organizer's calendar without guests, and invite the guests on approval.
3. **Spreadsheet bookkeeping in Excel**: append table rows and update ranges in workbooks on OneDrive or SharePoint, as Mycel does with Sheets today.
4. **Follow-up tasks** in To Do (personal) or Planner (team plans), created from mail or meetings.
5. **Teams notifications**: post a summary to a channel, or a message to a chat. Also, with admin consent, act on meeting transcripts after a meeting.

## 3. Pending forms

| Would-be external_send                               | Native pending form                                                                                                 | prepare / approve / decline / check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Outlook email (new, reply, forward)**              | **A draft in Drafts**, the direct counterpart of the Gmail draft                                                    | **prepare**: `POST /me/messages/{id}/createReply` (or `createReplyAll` / `createForward`, or `POST /me/messages` for a new mail) with `Prefer: IdType="ImmutableId"`; store the immutable ID [S13][S15]. **approve**: optional `PATCH` for edits, then `POST /me/messages/{id}/send` [S14]. **decline**: `DELETE /me/messages/{id}` (moves to Deleted Items). **check**: `GET /me/messages/{id}?$select=isDraft,parentFolderId`. Mycel's own send also flips `isDraft`, so the review must record that it sent the draft. The Sent Items copy may appear with a delay [S15]. |
| **Shared-mailbox email (support@)**                  | A draft in **the shared mailbox's** Drafts                                                                          | As above, on `/users/{shared}/messages/...` with `Mail.ReadWrite.Shared`, sending with `Mail.Send.Shared` and `from` = the shared address. | **Calendar invitation**                              | **An event on the organizer's calendar with no attendees** (same as the Google design)                              | **prepare**: `POST /me/events` without `attendees` (optionally `isOnlineMeeting: true`). **approve**: `PATCH /me/events/{id}` with `attendees`; Graph sends the request to changed attendees only [S16]. **decline**: `DELETE /me/events/{id}` (no attendees, so no cancellation email [S17]). **check**: `GET /me/events/{id}?$select=attendees`: no attendees → `waiting`; attendees present that Mycel didn't add → `done_in_app`; 404 → `gone`. Mechanics:

- **Validation.** On create, Graph POSTs to `notificationUrl?validationToken=…` and expects the token echoed in plain text. Each notification must get a 2xx within **3 s**. An endpoint becomes "slow" when over 10% of responses exceed 3 s in 10 min, and "drop" when over 15% exceed 10 s; dropped notifications can't be recovered. Failed deliveries retry for up to 4 h [S8].
- **Lifecycle notifications.** `reauthorizationRequired` (all resources), `subscriptionRemoved` (Outlook message, event and contact; Teams chatMessage) and `missed` (Outlook) go to `lifecycleNotificationUrl` [S7]. For delegated subscriptions, "if an access token expires, notifications aren't delivered" until the app reauthorizes, and retries last 4 h [S8]. Respond with `POST /subscriptions/{id}/reauthorize`, and renew with `PATCH /subscriptions/{id}` (`expirationDateTime`) [S5][S7]. Renewal is ours to schedule (Inngest cron), roughly daily for Outlook and every 2 days for Teams.
- **Rich notifications** (`includeResourceData`) need an encryption certificate and cut Outlook lifetimes to 1 day [S5]. Basic notifications (ID only) plus a live fetch fit invariant 8 better.
- **Immutable IDs in notifications.** Send `Prefer: IdType="ImmutableId"` when _creating_ the subscription [S15].
- **Subscription API limits**: 500 create/update/delete requests per 20 s per app per tenant, and only 25 GET-list requests per 20 s per app per tenant [S10].

**Polling: delta queries** [S9]

- Delta supports `message` (per folder), `mailFolder`, `event` (via `calendarView/delta`), `contact`, `driveItem`, `listItem`, `todoTask`, `todoTaskList` and `user`/`group`, plus `chatMessage` only app-only [S9][S30]. `plannerBucket` delta is beta only; `plannerUser` delta exists [S9].
- Token lifetime: Outlook tokens have **no fixed lifetime**; they expire when the internal token cache evicts them, returning a 40X with `syncStateNotFound`, so full resync is needed. Directory tokens last 7 days. Any delta can return `410 Gone` (resync) [S9].
- Recommended pattern: notification → delta (for OneDrive this is the only way to learn what changed).
- **Teams polling rule**: polling a resource more than once a day violates the API terms; use subscriptions [S27].

**For shared mailboxes:** delegated `.Shared` scopes can't subscribe [S19]. Poll `GET /users/{shared}/mailFolders/inbox/messages/delta` on an Inngest schedule. The delta page lists only `Mail.ReadBasic`/`Mail.Read`/`Mail.ReadWrite` [S45]; whether `.Shared` works for delta on another mailbox is **not documented** and needs a dogfood test. The documented fallback is listing with `$filter=receivedDateTime ge …`, as in [S19].

## 5. Auth

**One multitenant Entra app** with redirect to Nango, the `/common` authority (Nango's `microsoft` provider uses `login.microsoftonline.com/common` [S40]), delegated permissions only, plus `offline_access` for refresh tokens. Refresh tokens last **90 days** (sliding) for web apps [S39].

What this means:

- **Plan for one tenant-wide admin consent per customer.** The admin approves all delegated scopes once, either through `prompt=admin_consent` or the admin-consent endpoint, or through the tenant's admin consent workflow. After that, members connect without a prompt. That consent grants the app nothing app-only.
- **Publisher verification.** Multitenant apps registered after 2020-11-08 that aren't publisher-verified and request more than basic permissions trigger "risky" consent: users are stepped up to admin consent and see an "unverified" warning. Verification requires a Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MPN) account tied to the app [S3][S4]. Do it before M10. It costs no money, but takes paperwork. Microsoft's managed consent policy above applies **even to verified apps** for the listed scopes [S2].
- **Protected or app-only APIs (not needed in Mycel's model):** `/chats/getAllMessages`, `/teams/getAllMessages`, `/users/{id}/chats/getAllMessages` and chat delta are app-only [S28][S30]. Historically they also required Microsoft's protected-API request form. The standalone "protected APIs in Teams" page now returns 404, and the metered-API page (updated 2026-04-03) still says "some metered APIs … are protected and require additional validation … submit a request" [S33]. Teams APIs **stopped being metered on 2025-08-25** [S33]. App-only transcript access additionally needs a tenant _application access policy_ (Teams PowerShell) [S31]. Mycel's delegated design avoids all of this.
- **Incremental consent.** Graph allows asking for more scopes later. Following ADR-0014's "ask once" approach means requesting all of them up front. We found nothing in the docs showing Entra lets users untick individual scopes, as Google's granular consent does, so "record which apps were granted" matters less. The `scp` claim still shows what was granted (unverified behaviour; test).

## 7. Nango

Nango has many Microsoft providers. Most are **aliases of the generic `microsoft` provider** (same OAuth app, `graph.microsoft.com` proxy): `outlook`, `one-drive` (OneDrive for Business), `sharepoint-online` (v2), `microsoft-teams`, `microsoft-excel`, `microsoft-word`, `microsoft-planner`, `microsoft-people`, `microsoft-entra-id`, `microsoft-powerpoint` [S40]. Others:

- `microsoft-tenant-specific` (tenant-pinned authority);
- `microsoft-admin` (the `/adminconsent` flow);
- app-only `microsoft-oauth2-cc` and `microsoft-oauth2-cc-cert`, plus their `-oauth2-cc` aliases (non-goal 7: don't use);
- `one-drive-personal` (consumer);
- `sharepoint-online-v1` (legacy SharePoint REST);
- `microsoft-teams-bot` (Bot Framework, `smba.trafficmanager.net`) [S40].

Notable for Mycel:

- **One integration covers everything.** Every M365 app is the same host and token, so a single `microsoft` integration (mirroring ADR-0014's single `google` integration) with all scopes is the natural shape. `microsoft`'s `default_scopes` are `offline_access` + `.default` [S40]. `.default` requests whatever static permissions are on the app registration, so set explicit scopes on the integration instead. Nango also supports a per-connection `oauth_scopes_override` [S44].
- **Proxy**: base URL `https://graph.microsoft.com`, honors the `retry-after` header, handles decompression [S40].
- **Webhooks**: the `outlook` provider has a routing script. It echoes `validationToken`, checks each notification's `clientState` against the integration's `webhookSecret`, and maps notifications to connections by **`metadata.subscriptionIds`** on the connection. Then it forwards them [S41]. So Mycel must:
  1. create subscriptions itself through the proxy, with `notificationUrl` = Nango's webhook URL and `clientState` = the secret;
  2. write each subscription ID into the connection's metadata;
  3. renew and reauthorize them itself.

  Lifecycle events carry the same `subscriptionId`/`clientState`, so they likely route the same way; this is unverified. The `microsoft-teams` alias's routing script verifies **Bot Framework** tokens, which is for bots, not Graph notifications [S42]. For Teams Graph subscriptions, point them at the `outlook`-style route or at Mycel's own route handler.

- **Prebuilt actions and syncs**: `outlook` ships 28 actions (create-draft-message, send-mail, reply-to-message, create-event, etc.) and 4 syncs [S43]. Mycel doesn't use Nango syncs (architecture: no syncs), but the actions are a reference for Mycel's own catalog adapters.
- **No Nango test OAuth app** for Outlook; register our own Entra app [S43].

## 8. Shared mailboxes (the analogue of org connections like support@)

- A shared mailbox is created with an active user account and **a system-generated, unknown password**; Microsoft points admins to block sign-in for it [S20]. Connecting "as support@" (Mycel's Google pattern) would mean an admin resetting the password and enabling sign-in, which goes against Microsoft's guidance.
- The supported pattern is a **member's delegated connection** plus Exchange mailbox permissions on the shared mailbox:
  - **Full Access** (open, read, modify) and **Send As** (mail shows as from support@), or **Send on Behalf** ("Alice on behalf of Support") [S20][S18].
  - The Graph scopes are `Mail.Read.Shared` / `Mail.ReadWrite.Shared` for reads and drafts, and `Mail.Send.Shared` with `from` set to the shared address for sending [S18][S19].
  - Graph can't list which mailboxes a user has rights to, so the admin must type the shared address when setting up the org connection [S18].
- **Consequences for Mycel's model:**
  - An "org connection" for support@ becomes _a pointer to a shared mailbox, accessed through one or more members' Microsoft connections_. If that member disconnects or loses Full Access, the org source breaks. A fallback member, or "any member with Full Access", would help.
  - Reviews of support@ drafts: the draft lives in the shared mailbox's Drafts, so any Full Access member can see it and send it from Outlook too. That matches "first to act wins".
  - **No push triggers on the shared mailbox** with `.Shared` scopes; poll it [S19].
  - Deletion (invariant 3) keys on the member's `connection_id`, so the shared-mailbox source needs its own record so that removing one member doesn't orphan or wrongly delete org data.
- The only push route for a shared mailbox is app-only `Mail.Read` [S19], ideally scoped to that one mailbox with Exchange **RBAC for Applications** [S47]. That is admin-level and app-only, so it's a non-goal 7 decision to revisit, not a v1 path.

## 9. Gaps and gotchas

- **Word has no suggestion mode or comments in Graph.** The Docs-style pending form doesn't carry over (§3). Office.js has tracked changes but needs an installed add-in [S24].
- **Excel only works for `.xlsx` in business OneDrive or SharePoint**, not `.xls` or consumer OneDrive [S21]. Sessions expire after minutes [S21], and concurrent writes to one workbook are discouraged [S22].
- **Planner premium plans aren't in Graph** [S34]. Planner has no change notifications [S6].
- **OneNote has no change notifications or delta**, delegated only since 2025-03-31, and tight per-user limits [S36][S10].
- **Teams**: no drafts or scheduled sends [S29]; polling more than once a day breaks the terms [S27]; chat delta is app-only [S30]; reading chats and channels needs admin-consented or managed-policy-blocked scopes [S1][S2]; each user can have at most 10 user-level chat subscriptions [S6].
- **Transcripts**: delegated access needs admin consent. The tenant can disable Graph transcript access or speaker attribution (new controls from the end of July 2026). Subscriptions only catch transcripts that start after you subscribe [S31][S32].
- **Outlook IDs change on move** unless you opt into immutable IDs on every request and on subscription creation [S15].
- **Delete semantics**: deleting a meeting as organizer emails cancellations [S17], so a "decline" must only ever delete Mycel's attendee-less held events.
- **Send-as-shared** puts the Sent Items copy in the _member's_ mailbox unless you send through `/users/{shared}`, which needs Full Access [S18].
- **Sharing**: `createLink` with no scope uses the tenant's default link type, which may be anonymous [S26]. Always pass `scope` explicitly.
- **Licensing**: all of this needs Exchange Online, SharePoint/OneDrive and Teams licenses in the tenant (Business Basic and above). Shared mailboxes need no license. This is general Microsoft 365 knowledge; we didn't verify a specific plan matrix.
- **Unknowns worth a dogfood test**: delta on a shared mailbox with `.Shared` scopes; re-responding to a silently tentatively-accepted meeting; whether the consent screen lets users drop individual scopes; Nango routing of lifecycle notifications.
